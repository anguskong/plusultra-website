Privacy policy

Plus Ultra is a local training journal. We do not receive your workout data.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

What stays on your device

The app stores your exercises, routines, programs, workout history, notes, training profile and preferences locally using Apple’s SwiftData and UserDefaults. You can use it without an account.

No tracking or analytics

The app has no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs or developer-operated server. It does not collect device identifiers, usage analytics or location and does not request Health read access. We do not sell or share your training data.

Optional Apple Health and Watch

Apple Health workout export is off until you enable it and grant write permission. Plus Ultra exports completed workout times and a general activity classification, without heart rate, location, exercise names, notes or estimated calories. It requests no Health read access. While export is enabled, correcting or deleting local history updates or removes its exported workout. Turning export off leaves existing Health workouts in place; manage those records and permissions in Health. Consent and export bookkeeping stay device-local and are excluded from complete training archives and system device backups. The optional Apple Watch companion exchanges training with the paired iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity. Core training works without a Watch or Health permission.

Backups and deletion

Profile → Workout data exports complete .plusultra archives and workout CSV files. Files are processed locally. You choose their destination; a cloud drive or other app you select may transfer them under its own policy. Restoring a complete archive replaces local training after preview and confirmation. Device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Plus Ultra does not provide its own cloud sync. You can delete individual routines, custom exercises and workouts in the app. To remove all local app data and preferences, delete the app through iOS Settings. Offloading preserves its data. Manage previously created backups separately in your Apple settings.

Optional music links

A saved playlist link stays in app preferences. Opening it hands the link to Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube Music through iOS. The service processes that request under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your listening activity or music account information.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request. Please avoid sharing private workout or health information. Contact: .

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics, advertising or external fonts. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and access logs to deliver the site. Those requests do not include your local app training records.

Policy updates

We will update the date above when this policy changes. You can also read the app’s bundled policy offline in Profile → Privacy Policy.
