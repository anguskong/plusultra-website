Getting started

Choose your goal, experience and units during onboarding. Pick a routine on Today, adjust it for this session if needed, then start and log each set. Close an unfinished workout and resume it from Today.

Frequently asked questions

What devices are supported?

Plus Ultra requires iOS 26.2 or later on iPhone or iPad.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Training works offline without an account. Opening a playlist link uses an external music service and may require internet access or that service’s account.

Can I correct a saved workout?

Open Progress, then workout history. Choose a workout and tap Edit. Only completed sets count toward performed-work summaries; unlogged sets stay unlogged.

Where is my data? Can I move it between devices?

Your journal is stored locally. In Profile → Workout data, export a complete .plusultra backup, choose where to save it, and restore it on another device after reviewing the preview. Restoring replaces current local training after confirmation. Workout CSV import/export is available there too. Plus Ultra has no dedicated cloud sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Read the privacy policy before deleting or replacing a device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual routines, custom exercises and workouts in the app. Delete the app through iOS Settings to remove all local app data and preferences; offloading keeps the data. Manage existing backups separately in your Apple settings.

Report an issue

Include your app version (Profile → About), device model, iOS version, what you expected and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots can help; remove any private information before sending.

Training suggestions are general fitness information. Choose loads and movements appropriate to your ability and stop if you feel pain.
